🧠 Therapy App – README

Welcome to the Therapy App repository! This project is a simple yet powerful web application designed to help therapists promote their private practice. Whether you're a seasoned professional or just starting out, this app offers a user-friendly platform to showcase your services and connect with clients.

🚀 Live Demo
Experience the app in action here: https://www.trishbourke-therapy.ie/

💡 Features
Therapist Profile: Display your qualifications, specialties, and contact information.

Service Listings: Highlight the types of therapy you offer, such as individual sessions, couples therapy, or group workshops.

Testimonials: Share client feedback to build trust and credibility.

Blog Section: Post articles or resources related to mental health and wellness.

🛠️ Technologies Used
Frontend: React.js
Styling: CSS / Styled Components

📦 Installation
Clone the repository:
```git clone https://github.com/AndrewDonnelly93/therapy-app.git
cd therapy-app

Install dependencies:
npm install

Start the development server:
npm start

```

🧑‍⚕️ About Trish Bourke

Trish Bourke is a fully accredited psychotherapist (IACP M12324) based in Ireland. She offers integrative and person-centred therapy, specializing in areas such as:

- Anxiety
- Stress Management
- Depression
- Addiction
- Bereavement and Loss
- Relationship Issues
- Self-Esteem
- Work-Life Balance

Trish combines various therapeutic approaches, including Gestalt and Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), to provide personalized care for individuals aged 12 and above. Sessions are available online, ensuring accessibility for clients across Ireland.

For more information or to schedule a session, visit https://www.trishbourke-therapy.ie.

🧩 Integrating the App with Trish Bourke Therapy
This app can serve as a digital extension of Trish Bourke's therapy practice. By integrating the app with her existing website, clients can:

- Learn about the services offered
- Read informative blog posts

This integration enhances the client experience by providing a seamless online presence that complements in-person sessions.

🧠 Final Thoughts
The Therapy App is more than just a website—it's a tool to help therapists grow their practice and connect with clients in meaningful ways. Whether you're looking to expand your online presence or streamline your booking process, this app provides the foundation to do so.

Feel free to contribute, customize, and make it your own. Together, we can make mental health support more accessible to those who need it most.
